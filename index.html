About Us

One heart. Many possibilities.

Women of One Heart Initiative is a Ugandan community-focused organisation established to uplift, inspire and empower women and young girls through practical skills, knowledge, confidence and opportunity.

We believe women and girls have enormous potential to contribute to Uganda's social, technological and economic development. Our work helps bridge access gaps through motivation, mentorship, ICT, engineering and hands-on learning.

Our Vision

A society in which every woman and young girl has the skills, confidence and opportunities to realise her full potential.

Our Mission

To empower women and young girls through motivation, mentorship, practical skills training and access to technology.

Our programmes combine hands-on technical training with mentorship and personal development, giving participants skills they can apply in work, enterprise and community problem-solving.

Building confidence in the digital world, from foundational literacy to software development.

  • Software development
